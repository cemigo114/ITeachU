About Us

Cognality is building the next generation of assessment for the age of AI.

For decades, assessments have relied on answers, scores, and finished products to measure learning and ability. But in an AI-powered world, those outputs no longer provide enough evidence of what someone actually understands or can do.

We believe assessment should capture thinking in action.

Our platform uses carefully designed AI conversations to help learners explain ideas, defend reasoning, solve authentic problems, collaborate with others, and teach what they know. These interactions provide rich evidence of understanding that traditional assessments cannot capture.

Today, we are transforming assessment in K–12 education through products that identify learning gaps, orchestrate productive discussions, and reveal demonstrated competence. Tomorrow, the same technology will support talent acquisition, workforce development, professional certification, and lifelong learning.

Because the future of assessment isn't about collecting better answers, it's about understanding people more deeply.
