Our Vision

A world where important decisions are informed by how people think, not just what they produce.

We envision a future where learning, competence, and potential are understood through meaningful interactions rather than isolated answers. From classrooms to workplaces, people should have the opportunity to demonstrate what they truly know and can do.

Our Mission

To build the next generation of assessment through AI-powered conversations that reveal thinking, deepen understanding, and demonstrate competence.

By combining learning science, assessment science, and artificial intelligence, we create meaningful interactions that uncover learning gaps, foster productive collaboration, and provide richer evidence for educational and professional decision making.
